Chonkie is a lightweight ingestion library for RAG pipelines. It turns long documents into small, meaningful chunks before they are embedded and stored.

Chunking is the first step of every RAG pipeline. A good chunk should be self-contained, reconstructable, and large enough to be meaningful. Bad chunks lead to bad retrieval, which leads to bad answers.

Chonkie ships with several chunkers and refineries. Refineries add extra context to chunks after splitting, so a chunk can stand on its own during retrieval. The library also provides a Pipeline API that wires these components together.
